dwdash-rclone Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Who uses this app

dwdash-rclone is used only by the owner of the Google account it is connected to. It is not available to anyone else.

What data it accesses

The app requests the drive.file scope only. With this scope it can see and change only the files that the app itself created in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot read any other files in the Drive.

How the data is used

The app uploads the owner's backup files to the owner's own Google Drive, and reads those same files back when the owner restores or checks a backup.

What it does not do

  • It does not store anyone's data outside the owner's Google Drive.
  • It does not share data with anyone.
  • It does not sell data.
  • It does not collect data about anyone other than the owner.
